The campaign in three parts

A gift today.
A habit for tomorrow.

Shared Values is an Adam Foundation campaign inspired by Avicenna Scholars. It brings communities together around care, responsibility and cooperation.

Kawthar is its reusable bottle initiative: a practical gift, a clear reason to reuse it and a connection to the wider community.

Give something useful

A reusable bottle, shared as a gift, gives someone a practical way to start refilling and keep using it every day.

Explain the better choice

Show why disposable waste adds up, where to refill and how to keep the habit going. Help people understand the reason behind the gift.

Keep the connection going

The bottle’s QR code connects its owner with Community Exchange Hub, Adam Foundation and opportunities to take part beyond the campaign.

Why this campaign matters

“It’s just one bottle.”
But the habit adds up.

Every disposable bottle has a life before and after the drink.

The disposable habit

Used once.
The journey continues.

  1. 01 · MAKE

    Resources go in.

    Making plastic bottles uses raw materials, often oil and gas, and energy.

  2. 02 · REPLACE

    The cycle repeats.

    Take a new disposable bottle each time, and the demand continues.

  3. 03 · DISCARD

    Waste adds up.

    Every discarded bottle needs managing. Litter can reach rivers and seas.

A habit worth keeping

Keep it. Refill it. Repeat.

  1. 01Refill
  2. 02Use
  3. 03Bring back

Each refill that replaces a disposable bottle means one fewer bottle to throw away.

Amanah in everyday life

Amanah.
Caring for what we’ve been entrusted with.

In Islam, Amanah means a trust—and the responsibility to honour it. Kawthar brings that principle into everyday life: caring for resources, serving others and thinking about what our choices leave behind.

What we have is a trust

Honour the trust

Amanah

For Kawthar, this means looking after what we have, using resources responsibly and treating the people we serve with care.

Value the blessing

Shukr · gratitude

From COP31
to everyday life.

Adam Foundation plans to launch Shared Values from COP31, with Avicenna Scholars helping bring the call for collective action to UK communities.

The Kawthar bottle initiative turns that message into something practical for Ramadan 2027: a useful gift, an explanation of why reuse matters and encouragement to keep refilling.

Your organisation can help take that message further through education, communication, distribution or team involvement.

Distribution is planned to begin in January 2027. An independent Adam Foundation campaign.
