The disposable habit

Used once.
The journey continues.

  1. 01 · MAKE

    Resources go in.

    Making plastic bottles uses raw materials, often oil and gas, and energy.

  2. 02 · REPLACE

    The cycle repeats.

    Take a new disposable bottle each time, and the demand continues.

  3. 03 · DISCARD

    Waste adds up.

    Every discarded bottle needs managing. Litter can reach rivers and seas.

The name behind your bottles

Why Kawthar?

Kawthar means abundant goodness. In Islamic tradition, it is also the name of a river in Paradise.

Surah Al-Kawthar ↗
Sahih al-Bukhari 4964 ↗
Are the bottles free for worshippers?

Yes. The bottles are intended to be given to worshippers free of charge.

Can we apply before arranging a refill point?

Yes. Tell us what you have in place and where you need help. Access to safe drinking water is important so people can keep using their bottles.

Will we receive the number we request?

Requests are reviewed against available stock and distribution arrangements. Applying does not guarantee a particular quantity.
