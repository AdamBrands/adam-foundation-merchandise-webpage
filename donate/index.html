A shared idea.

The idea behind Our Planet, Our Responsibility came from Avicenna Scholars: bring communities together around a simple change they can make every day.

Adam Foundation plans to launch the campaign from COP31. Kawthar puts the idea into practice by encouraging people to replace disposable bottles with a bottle they keep and refill.

Bottle distribution is planned to begin in January 2027, ahead of Ramadan. An independent Adam Foundation initiative.

About the appeal

A few useful answers

What does my donation fund?

Your donation contributes towards reusable bottles and their distribution. Participating Masjids will arrange safe drinking water for refills.

What does the progress indicator show?

It shows the bottles funded, starting with Adam Foundation’s confirmed contribution. Bottles delivered are tracked separately.

Can my donation help create lasting Khayr?

Yes. You can give with the intention of seeking Allah’s reward and helping others through a gift they can continue using. Reward rests with Allah; the campaign does not promise a fixed reward per bottle or refill.

How do I pay?

Choose an amount, add your details, then pay on a secure page run by Stripe. We never see your card details, and your receipt is emailed to you.
