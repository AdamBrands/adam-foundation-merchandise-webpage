Kawthar · Part of Our Planet, Our Responsibility

The story behind your bottle.

Your Kawthar bottle is part of Our Planet, Our Responsibility, an Adam Foundation campaign inspired by Avicenna Scholars. It brings a simple idea into daily life: keep a bottle, refill it and help reduce disposable waste.

Each refill is a small way to care for the planet we share. Stay connected with the people and projects helping communities take the next step.
